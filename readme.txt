X-SUMMIT BIKES

Integrated Business Planning Simulation

Authors

Murat Kristal Işık Biçer

Schulich School of Business York University

OVERVIEW

X-Summit Bikes is an Integrated Business Planning (IBP) simulation
designed to place participants in the role of an executive team
responsible for managing a new product line under uncertainty.

Participants make interconnected decisions across pricing, demand,
inventory, operations, and finance. The simulation demonstrates how
commercial and operational decisions jointly affect revenue,
profitability, service performance, operating efficiency, inventory
exposure, and capital discipline.

Rather than optimizing a single objective, participants must manage the
business as an integrated decision system while responding to changing
forecasts and market information.

SIMULATION EXPERIENCE

The simulation operates across multiple planning years and monthly
decision cycles. Participants work with annual operating commitments and
rolling demand information while making pricing, replenishment,
inventory, and operating decisions.

Decisions have financial and operational consequences that accumulate
throughout the simulation. Participants must therefore balance
short-term responses with annual commitments and longer-term business
performance.

CORE DECISION AREAS

Pricing — Participants evaluate whether and when pricing should change
in response to demand conditions, inventory exposure, commercial
commitments, and the remaining planning horizon.

Demand — Rolling forecasts and new market information must be
interpreted carefully. Participants determine whether new information
materially changes the decision-relevant demand outlook.

Inventory — Replenishment and inventory decisions require balancing
product availability against holding costs, excess inventory exposure,
and potential year-end write-offs.

Operations — Operating decisions influence the firm’s ability to support
commercial objectives while remaining within the annual operating plan.

Finance — Participants must consider the financial consequences of their
decisions, including revenue, margins, operating costs, inventory
investment, and overall capital discipline.

LEARNING OBJECTIVE

X-Summit Bikes is designed to demonstrate that Integrated Business
Planning cannot be reduced to isolated functional decisions. Strong
performance requires Sales, Supply Chain, Operations, and Finance to
operate as one coordinated decision system.

The simulation emphasizes decision-making under uncertainty,
cross-functional trade-offs, disciplined planning, and the financial
consequences of operational choices.

INSTRUCTOR AND PLAYER EXPERIENCE

Instructors can create and configure private simulation environments,
establish player flexibility settings, distribute invitation links,
monitor participant progress, review individual results, and evaluate
cohort performance.

Players join simulations through instructor-provided invitations. After
registration, participants access their assigned simulations through the
Simulation Lobby and progress through the planning years and final
results summary.
