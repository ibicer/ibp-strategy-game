Management Reflection

What the two years tested

Year 1 · Alignment

Build a disciplined operating baseline.

Year 1 focused on understanding the mechanics of the Annual Operating Plan and translating those commitments into monthly S&OP decisions. Forecasting, base-stock replenishment, pricing, sales credit, budget consumption, and fixed-cost absorption had to remain aligned.

Year 2 · Adaptation

Use pricing and supply decisions selectively.

Year 2 increased the growth ambition and widened demand uncertainty. New market information required you to distinguish between a signal that merely appeared and a signal that actually changed the economics of the inventory decision. Pricing became an S&OP lever that had to be coordinated with replenishment and commercial commitments.

Final Integrated Business Planning Reflection

The objective was never simply to maximize profit.

Integrated Business Planning connects the Annual Operating Plan with recurring Sales & Operations Planning. Sales shapes demand and brings orders into the business. Supply Chain converts those orders into fulfilled revenue. Finance establishes the economic boundaries within which both must operate. Strong management coordinates all three rather than optimizing one function in isolation.
